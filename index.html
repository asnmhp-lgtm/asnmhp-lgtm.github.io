bontai uploader

A private publishing tool · 个人自用的视频上传工具

bontai uploader is a personal tool used by its owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (bontai and Cindy) through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

它只用来把机主自己做的视频上传到机主自己的 YouTube 频道，并设置标题、简介、标签、可见性和播放列表。
