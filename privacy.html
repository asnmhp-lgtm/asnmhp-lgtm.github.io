Privacy Policy

bontai uploader · Last updated: 2026-10-04

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app can read basic information about the owner's YouTube channel and upload and manage videos and playlists on that channel (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl, youtube.readonly).

How the data is used

The access is used only to upload the owner's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and visibility, add them to playlists, and read their processing status. Nothing else is read or changed.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be left on the project's GitHub issues page.
